Rare (trips mostly walk) but "members in danger are not left behind" is not strictly true for trips. Suggest `ovEngineParty(p) && s.Trip && !memberInDanger(s)`. Follow-up, not a blocker.
- minor, together.go:76-80 (collectLagDue): the clock is not cleared when a collect completes; a lag reappearing within 5 s of the last sighting is due at once. Harmless.

## Fixer's flagged points
- (a) isAyla (exchange.go:226, chatintent.go:136, starveillead.go:389) keys G12 on the name "Ayla". G12/AY09 name Ayla by definition, so it is not a breach, but it is a name-keyed special case under G25/PR16 and breaks if her character is renamed or another engine-party human is added. Decision: file a follow-up bug (minor): key the floor on enginePartyOn(charName) or a tuning field. Not blocking this branch.
- (b) NPC shop purchases (town.go ~1205 potion/arrow budget, ~1324 gear buy `pr <= b.Zeny-fare`) can spend her zeny below 10,000z. G12/AY09 forbids "sending away" zeny (mind, aylagear top-ups, guild funding, gifts, deals); a shop purchase for her own survival and gear is spending, not sending, and the rule text does not cover it. The potion and arrow budget keeps a 500z reserve and is a survival need. Decision: not a G12 breach as written; do not change it in this branch. Ask the owner whether the floor should also hold for her own gear buys (town.go:1324); file a bug to track that question. Out of scope for this fix.
- (c) Member side waits up to 30 s (party.go:793) only while the leader's collect is in force (Collect == its name), which now starts after the 10 s grace; during the grace the member regroups on its own. The two do not conflict: one side moves at a time, the member's wait resets when it approaches. No change needed.

No blocker, no major.

verdict: merge
