## Correctness
Bug b1794746339362-336: four party members Butterfly-winged to Payon at 10-28% HP with the priest on their map. Fix: in
`stickEscape` (`legion/partystick.go:222-244`) a member with a healer on its map (40 cells, any distance in a `_dun`
map) or a potion drinks, calls for a heal (20 s gap, party chat, no mention of bots) and runs to the healer; the wing
stays only for no healer and no potion. The root cause (the wing was the first branch regardless of the priest) is
fixed, not a symptom. `stickHealer` counts a healer bot as its own healer, skips offline and other-map members and bad
coordinates.

## Findings
- minor, `legion/partystick.go:230`: `hok || pot >= 0` means any bot carrying one potion never wings, also when
  `deadly` is true (a far stronger monster) and no healer is near. A Red Potion heals little at level 80 and drinking
  is one item per second, so a deadly mob can still kill it where the wing would have saved it. The QA case was the
  healer case; the potion-only case is a judgement call. Suggest requiring a healer for the `deadly` case, or a potion
  that heals a meaningful share of max HP. Not proven harmful live, so minor.
- minor, `legion/partystick.go:236-238`: the heal request is spoken even when the healer is far (dungeon, any
  distance); the bot runs the whole way at under 20% HP. Intended by the comment; watch deaths.
- minor, tests: `TestStickEscapeNoWingWithHealer` (`partystick_test.go:192`) covers healer present, potion only and the
  remaining wing; it fails on the old code. It does not cover `deadly=true`, a healer bot itself, or the 20 s call gap.
