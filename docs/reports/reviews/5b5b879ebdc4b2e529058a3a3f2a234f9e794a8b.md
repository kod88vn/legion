## Findings

### minor 1: leash has no cooldown and no hysteresis (protectsupport.go:154-160, 188-190)
A leashed member (18+ cells, not hit) returns true every tick and zeroes its target, until it is under 18. Probe: a ranged
member at 19 cells assisting a target at 23 had its target zeroed, and 10 ticks in 2 s gave 10 moves. At the exact edge
the normal flow (assist fight outward) and the hook (walk back one cell) can alternate for a few ticks; it is bounded by
distance, not by time. Suggest the leash walk to under 15, or the same cooldown. Not harmful enough to block.

### minor 2: engagedNow skip is unproven (protectsupport_test.go)
Disabling the `engagedNow` check fails no test. The claim "never zeroes a target in melee" is true on my manual probe
(target kept, hook not fired) but should have a test.

### minor 3: the walk can be taken over after one tick
The cooldown is set on the first tick, so while the member is still walking (moving) the next ticks fall through to the
normal flow, which can send it elsewhere. A cost of one hook walk per 3 s, never a loop.

SUPPORT (her priest); Ayla is not the support (isHealer is the Acolyte family), so the members that follow her are covered
and Ayla herself needs no move. Gaps for a lone support target and the support's death stay as the audit lists them.
supportOf picks the nearest support, so two healers are handled.
