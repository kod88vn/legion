## Findings

### major 1: the hook preempts the tank's own jobs and can zero its fight (party.go:1015, protectsupport.go:135-137)
The hook sits before tankFallBack, protectSupport, tankTakeAggro (Provoke), tankGoHelp and meleePeel. When it fires it sets
b.target = 0 and returns true, so a tank that is more than 2 cells from the guard spot loses its current target and its
Provoke/peel on every tick until it is within 2 cells of the spot. The only exception is a target already within 2 cells of
the member. AY26's own existing mechanisms (the audit table) are overridden instead of complemented: a monster already on
the support is taken off it by protectSupport only if the tank first reaches the spot.

### major 2: ping-pong and kills-per-minute cost, never tested (protectsupport.go:96-110)
supportThreat fires for ANY monster within 8 cells of the support, aggressive or not. While hunting, the support is
almost always within 8 of some monster. A frontline at the guard spot (<= 2) falls through to the normal flow, pulls a
monster 3+ cells away, steps off the spot, and the next tick the hook walks it back and zeroes the target: the fixer's own
ping-pong flag. A ranged member steps back whenever any monster is within 6 of it and within 8 of the support, which is
the normal state of a ranged hunter at range 5-9, so it is pulled out of its firing position and loses its target every
tick. The leash (15 cells to the support) is tighter than tankPullOK range (togetherCells-4 = 16 from the leader), so pulls
and fights at the edge of that range drop the target and walk back. Expect a kills/min drop on the party; the audit lists
this as "needs live evidence" but nothing bounds it (no cooldown, no hysteresis, no "only when the monster targets the
support or is aggressive" test).

### major 3: no off switch for an engine party (protectsupport.go:40-45)
partyProtectOn is flag OR engine party, so an engine party (Ayla's party in the engine era) cannot be turned off by the
tuning flag if major 2 shows up live; the flag only adds bot parties. PR40 wants Ayla's party first, but a flag that
cannot disable the behaviour there gives no revert short of a deploy. Add a second flag (or let a "off" value win).

### major 4: the tests do not prove the hook (protectsupport_test.go)
All tests call protectSpot, guardSpot, supportLeashed or protectOwnSupport directly. Nothing runs partyThink. Mutation:
deleting the party.go:1015-1017 hook (or moving it) fails no test. No test covers supportThreat with real entities, the
frontline/ranged branches of protectOwnSupport with a mob, sitting/stand, the "being hit" skip, the "in melee, keep
fighting" skip, or the interplay with tankTakeAggro/meleePeel. No test holds b.mu or a member lock with a timeout (the
brief for this review asked for one; the code is lock-clean but unproven). Only the leash path of protectOwnSupport is run.

### minor 5: coverage gaps stated honestly in the audit, not closed
- The bot party leader is outside the member flow (ai.go hunt, audit gap 6): in an engine party the leader is the bot pinned
  to Ayla's character, so Ayla herself is NOT covered by the member flow when she leads; only her four members are. The
  other four protect her only if she is the support, which she is not (isHealer is Acolyte family). Leashing members to
  "the support" is therefore the priest's, fine, but Ayla leading an engine party is unmoved by AY26.
- Gap 5 (the support as a lone target) and 7 (support death) are left open; AY26's "no one leaves the support behind" is
  met for members only.
- supportOf takes the first online Support in the snapshot (a human lead is appended last); with two healers the choice is
  by list order.

### minor 6: details
- protectsupport.go:135 sets b.target = 0 even for the leash case when the member is mid-walk: harmless, same as regroup.
- protectsupport.go:92 guardSpot can be a non-walkable cell; stepAlong's path to a blocked goal is unchecked here
  (guardSpot/backSpot never test gm.Walkable). Possible endless path failures near walls.
- party.go: CRLF kept (diff shows five clean lines). gofmt listing includes party.go and tuning.go already on main.

## Rules and flag policy
Constitution and gates: nothing touches Ayla's AI, live data or a deploy. Default OFF for non-engine parties (good);
engine parties are on by default by design (majors 2 and 3 matter exactly there). No new behaviour for other parties.

## Verdict reasoning
Locks are safe and the off path is cheap, but the hook as written can stop a frontline killing (target zeroed ahead of the
tank's own aggro and peel logic) and pull ranged out of position on every tick in the one party that cannot switch it off,
with no test that runs the hook. Needs: fire only on a real threat (aggressive mob targeting the support or about to),
hysteresis or a cooldown, hook after tankTakeAggro/protectSupport/meleePeel (or leave the target alone), a way to switch
the engine party off, and a partyThink-level test (mutation-proof) with a mob entity.

verdict: do not merge
