## Major

1. together.go:1085-1097 (h90, PT04/PT32b): the new gate makes a normal one-floor lag a walk-out. When she steps from
   pay_dun00 to pay_dun01 the member still on pay_dun00 is "alone on its floor" (friendOnFloor false, same family), so
   escortGate returns the entrance town with "gathering before going in". The member walks out of the dungeon, then
   waits in town for the others (outside/inTown logic, 1117-1126) while she goes deeper. Every portal she takes can
   trigger this for every member a few seconds behind her. Walking out across the floors it is on is the same exposure
   as walking one floor forward (the QA deaths were on pay_dun00-02 either way), and it is the longest trip back.
   Better: a member alone on a floor holds on that floor (or at the portal/arrival point, `waiting for <leader>`), or
   follows to the leader's floor when it is exactly the neighbour floor and the leader/friend is there. Return to town only
   when the member is really hurt or out of potions. At minimum, exempt the adjacent floor (leader seen on the next
   floor within the last minutes) so ordinary following is unchanged.
2. together.go:1085: a member deeper than the leader (or on any floor with the leader on another floor of the same family)
   is also sent to the entrance town, even if the leader is one floor above and could be met by one portal. Same
   remedy as 1.
