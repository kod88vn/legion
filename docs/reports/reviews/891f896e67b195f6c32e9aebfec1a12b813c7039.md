## Findings
### minor: emergency stack ignores the wing reserve and the wing saving floor (town.go ~1412, potBuyPlanEm)
`zlim` only keeps 500z and the survival minimum. At 3,000z a lv85 bot spends 900z on Yellow although `wingSaveFloor` (~4,400z
at lv85) is meant to stay in the purse; wings are bought from what is left. The budget cap (1,000z floor) bounds this, and the
top-up already ignored the same floor only for the minimum. Acceptable, but the Yellow stack can starve Butterfly Wings on a
poor bot.

### minor: top-up is cut by the planned emergency zeny even when arrows or SP shrink the emergency buy (town.go ~1217)
The emergency is planned (emN) before arrows/SP are known; arrows/SP then take zeny and the actual `n` can fall below 3 (or 0),
while the top-up has already lost `emN*emPr` of the potion budget. Result: slightly fewer potions that restock, not unsafe.

### minor: emergency potions are outside the shared weight budget (`wLeft`) (town.go ~1431)
Only `potRoom` (pre-purchase weight) limits them. 5 Yellow weigh about 50, negligible against the 40% cap, so no real effect.

### minor: emergency walk ignores carried stronger-tier potions when they would not fit
A bot carrying 2 White and unable to afford 3 more falls to Yellow x5. Harmless; it still holds the White.
