## What it does
`ayla.sh tell` queues an order in `legion/data/ayla_orders.jsonl`; the dispatcher wakes the `ayla` helper within about
1 s (`owner_due`, 30 s floor, past min_gap and the hourly cap), the order heads the briefing, the helper reports with
`ayla.sh done`, and orders it did not report are closed by `close_unreported`.

  strip `;;` in `send()`, and restore the map regex. Not a defect proven live, hence minor.

  brief says "only for an owner order". Enforced by prose only. Owner reversed AY05 on 2026-11-14, so accepted.
