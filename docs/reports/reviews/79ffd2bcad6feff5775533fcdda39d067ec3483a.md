## Findings

### minor 1: the Meteor cooldown change is correct but nearly inert, and the mismatch it targets is wider (aoe.go:48, aoe.go:338, aoe_test.go TestMeteorCooldownIsTheServersPerLevel)
`b.buffAt = now + max(ms, cooldown)`. For Meteor `ms` is 6910, so `max` is 6910 for levels 1-9 (cooldown <= 6500) and 7000 only at
level 10: the change adds 90 ms at level 10 and nothing below. More important, rAthena starts the cooldown when the cast ends
(skill.cpp:5336-5341, `skill_blockpc_start` in the cast-end handler) while the bot's timer starts when it sends the cast. With a
real cast of about 3.6 s the server is blocked until about 3.6 + 7 = 10.6 s but the bot may recast at 7.0 s; a refusal (0x0110)
then costs a 60 s skip of that spell (bot.go:780). The same holds for Storm Gust (6.5 s against about 9.6 s) and Lord of
Vermilion, so this is pre-existing and not made worse by the change. The test proves the table, not "the server never refuses".
Meteor is third in `aoeSpells` and is chosen only when Storm Gust and Lord of Vermilion are not ready or are resisted, so Meteor 10
does not expose much more of it. Not a blocker. Follow-up: base the bot's timer on cast end + cooldown, and watch
`/api/skillaudit` refused counts for skills 83, 89, 85.

### minor 2: wizard_frost carries dead points (builds.go wizard_frost Skills)
Lightning Bolt 10 (4 are needed for Thunder Storm) and Cold Bolt 5 (the Frost Diver prerequisite) are not cast above that:
`casterSpell` prefers Jupitel unless the monster resists Wind, and Wind-resisting monsters get Fire Bolt or Earth Spike. About 6
Lightning Bolt points could be spent elsewhere; Legacy already permits LB 10, so G11 does not need it in `Skills`. The plan's
Summary still reads "Lord of Vermilion and Cold Bolt". In wizard main, Cold Bolt 6 is one point of filler to reach exactly 39 (fine).

### minor 3: wizard_frost leaves no Sightrasher / Meteor / Fire Wall (builds.go wizard_frost)
Design choice (Fire Ball 10 takes the last 9 points; aoe.go casts it). Acceptable; a Frost Wizard has no Fire Wall kiting. Noted.

### minor 4: the new plans are on by default, not behind a tuning flag
A plan-data rebalance like the earlier ones (EC42); characters adopt the new goals as they spend points and the Legacy caps keep
earlier characters valid (replay above). Recorded, not a harm.

## SP at the milestone levels (server data)
Base SP (db/re/job_basepoints.yml, Wizard): 550 at lv60, 640 at lv70, 730 at lv80, 820 at lv90, 901 at lv99. With the usual
(100+INT)/100 multiplier (I could not find that line in status.cpp by grep, so the multiplier is not source-verified): INT 80 at
lv70-80 gives about 1,150-1,310, INT 99 at lv99 about 1,790, before gear. One rotation Storm Gust 78 + Lord of Vermilion 96 +
Meteor 64 = 238 SP; the engine reserve is max(15%, 30) (aoe.go:101). So a full bar holds about 4-5 rotations at lv70 and 6-7 at
lv99 (15-20 casts): fine for bursts on grouped monsters, then the wizard is SP-limited. Natural regeneration and SP potions were
not computed, so sustained farming is not verified. Storm Gust is first in the order and costs the same at level 1 and 10, so
the plan is sensible; Meteor 10 (64 SP) is the cheapest per hit of the three.

verdict: merge
