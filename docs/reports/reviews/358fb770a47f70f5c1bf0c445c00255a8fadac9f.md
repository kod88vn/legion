## Findings

### major (flag-on only; fix before the flag is switched on anywhere)
1. pricedyn.go interestedBuyers 76-96 + pricemodel.go Clear 140-150: any buyer with a want and at least 1 zeny counts as
   "interested", even when its willingness is far below the seller's floor. One broke bot with an open want drags EVERY
   seller's listing of that item to about the floor (test TestFlagOnVendPriceFollowsUrgencyAndBuyers proves it: a 200z
   buyer pulls the listing down). On a thin market that is the price collapse the audit says the floor guards against, but
   the floor is only half the reference for cards/valuable goods/drop-only gear, and the NPC sell value (about 40% of ref)
   for the rest. Fix: only count buyers whose willingness is at least the seller's Floor (a buyer who cannot pay what the
   seller would take is not a customer), and ideally weight the pull by how far the buyer's purse is from the floor. This
   also removes the seller conceding when no sale can happen. EC48 wants a poor desperate buyer to pull the price so it
   sells sooner, which needs a real sale to be possible.
2. wantBid has a side effect (pricedyn.go 105-115): `b.dynNeed` is one float per bot, overwritten by every wantBid call,
   including probes that post nothing (regenWanted calls econWant, browseShops calls spPotionWant and wantBid on cards).
   Sellers then read a need that may belong to another item. Store the need with the Want (Want.Need set at posting) and
   have interestedBuyers read that; the audit already names this (dynWantNeed 0.6 fallback "until passed through the want").

### minor
3. Data race (bot.go dynNeed, pricedyn.go 83): float64 written under b.mu, read under econ.mu only. A -race run in a
   concurrent test would flag it; on amd64 no tearing in practice. It is the same class as the existing unlocked
   w.Buyer.Zeny read (market.go pattern), so acceptable to match, but cleaner is to carry the need in the Want (finding 2),
   which removes the read of the buyer's struct for it.
4. wantCap (pricedyn.go) accepts a shop listing at willingness with a fixed need 0.6 and ignores the want's own Each: a buyer
   that posted a low bid will still pay up to about 0.98 x ref. Use max(Each, ...) or the want's stored need for consistency.
5. pricedyn.go header lines 15-16 say the want sites are "not wired yet"; they are wired in 358fb77. Fix the stale comment.
6. vendPriceDyn/askPriceDyn: `max(marketSupply, 0)` turns "unknown" (-1) into zero supply, so an item with demand and no
   supply data gets up to 1.6x scarcity. Use supply 1 (neutral) when unknown, or skip scarcity.
7. Buyers' reference ignores scarcity (the market factor is removed from priceOf when on; scarcity is only in asks), so
   demand moves what sellers ask but not what buyers offer. Acceptable for EC48 (one side moves the clearing price);
   note for tuning.
8. economy.go priceOf: the flag comment is stuck in front of the old `// market.go (batch 21)` comment; tidy.

## Model math and EC48
- Scarcity sqrt((d+1)/(s+1)) in [0.6,1.6], urgency floor 0.5, markdown 0.95^steps (6 max), jitter 0.97-1.03: bounded and
  monotone; Ask clamped [Floor, Ceil]. Tests cover each (pricemodel_test.go). Clear never goes outside [Floor, Ask].
- EC48 end to end: supply/demand (Ask), seller hurry (zeny, bags, time), buyer need and money (Willingness, Clear) are all
  present. Finding 1 is the one place where the intent (a poor desperate buyer pulls the price down so it sells sooner)
  overshoots into a price drop with no sale.
- The in-memory sim (TestSimMarketPricesMove, TestSimPoorDesperateBuyerSellsSooner) is meaningful for direction (supply
  lifts ask, hurry lowers ask, desperate poor buyer reaches a sale sooner) but partly circular: the sale test compares the
  listing to the same Willingness function, and time is faked with simAge. It does not test the collapse case (finding 1);
  add a test with a 1z buyer and a thin market asserting the listing stays above the floor-weighted level.
- Fixed prices left (audit G): NPC sell value floor, half-ref floor for valuables, NPC window 62-76% (EC15), used gear 93%
  of NPC (batch 18 measurement), Dietrich cap, fallback fixedStandardPrice, glut farm steering. All are item-db facts, owner
  rule EC15 or guard rails; none is a price a player sets, so no EC48 violation. Acceptable.
- Not covered by EC48 here and fine: stock age of bag goods (audit F4), NPC potion prices (EC47).

## What to measure first (Article 3.2)
With the flag off, record per-item median listing price, sale count, time-to-sell, zeny/h and kills/min per bot for a day as
the baseline. Then enable on Ayla's party only (2.8) and compare: median price vs reference per item, share of listings at
the floor (finding 1 shows up here first), time-to-sell, count of sales below 60% of reference, and the price spread
between sellers of the same item.
