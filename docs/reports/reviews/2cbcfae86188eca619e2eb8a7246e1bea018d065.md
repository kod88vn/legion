# Review of fix/rally-flap 2cbcfae (bug b1794773273kln)

Checked: diff of rally.go, rallysync.go, rallysync_test.go; `openkore-ayla/plugins/cmdfile/partylead.pl` and `statusout.pl`;
`tools/dev/gocheck.sh` in the worktree.

## Root cause
Right fix. The old `humanLeftRally` check ended the mirrored rally when her map differed from the town, but her status still
said `rally_town=payon` (rally from a field to the town, or the rally still set while she stepped out), so `humanRallySync`
re-mirrored it on the next tick. Removing the leave check and adding one-rally-per-call (`MirrorDone`) removes the loop, not
only its symptom.

## Her plugin vs the open concern (when does she clear rally_town?)
`status_rally` (partylead.pl:1407) is `$rally ? (town,x,y) : ()`, written every 3 s (and at once on a map change) by
statusout.pl:rally_json. Every exit from a rally goes through `end_rally`, which sets `$rally = undef` before anything else
(partylead.pl:660-668): 60 s at the Kafra (825), saved and waiting (842, 848, 862), everyone met (868), friends say met (1490),
mind cancel (168), no route (2648), her own 12 min timeout (733). She sets off only after one of these, so `rally_town` is
empty by then. The one case where the status stays set while she is away from town is a rally from a field going to the town
(or paused for a friend in danger, 737), and there the members correctly keep waiting at the Kafra. So the fixer's concern
only bites if her rally is stuck, and then her own cap is 720 s, the same as the engine's 12 min. Seriousness: minor.

## Findings
- minor, rally.go:333 (b1794773273kln): the engine timeout is counted from the mirror start (`r.At`), hers from her start, so the
  engine can outlast hers by the status lag (3 s plus a tick); harmless.
- minor, rallysync.go:50 (b1794773273kln): `MirrorDone` is cleared only when `rally_town` is empty in a status. A new call to
  the same town and cell that starts and ends between two 3 s writes, or a mind rally to the same Kafra right after a cancel
  (`$rallyNext = 0`, partylead.pl:185), would be seen as the old call and not mirrored. Rare, and the members then follow her
  by the normal party sync. No action needed.
- minor, test: `TestLeaderTalkRally` failed once in the full run of gocheck.sh (pass=341 fail=1) and passed in two reruns
  with the rally tests. It is timing-based (sleeps) and does not touch rally.go changes; flake under load, not caused by this
  commit.

## Rules, gates, side effects
- No gate touched (G05, G13, G19, G20 not involved; nothing is sent to Ayla). Only human-led parties (`p.Human != ""`) are
  affected; bot-led parties keep their own rally and timeouts, so no side effect on other parties or the Legion.
- It changes behaviour without a `tuning.json` flag, but it removes a bug path (the restart loop) and adds no new behaviour
  the owner did not ask for; the only new element is the 12 min cap, which equals her own cap.
- Tests: the replay of the flapping sequence and the timeout test exercise the new code; they cannot pass without it (the
  old code re-mirrored on the same status). `rallyleave_test.go` is removed together with its code. vet ok.

verdict: merge
