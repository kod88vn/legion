## Major
- guildrank.go:179-184 (guild wealth): a remembered owner is never forgotten. The fallback fires when the feed shows the char with an empty guild (`charGuild[c]==""`), which is exactly what a char that left or was kicked looks like (guildduty.go:257 sets `s.guild` from the live guild name). `grank.owner` is only ever added to and now also persisted (`Owners`), so an online vendor that left guild A and keeps its shop open is still counted as guild A's stock (and keeps its old guild's entry forever). It needs: drop the owner when the char is in the feed with an empty guild after a short grace (login lag), and prune owners of chars absent for long (same 24 h as funds). Sold out is fine: empty listings add 0.
- guildrank.go:179-184 vs the bug text: `econ.shops` is built only from online snaps (economy.go:757-761, guildduty.go:240), so an offline vendor's shop is not in `econ.shops` at all. The owner memory therefore does not change stock for "vendors offline"; it only covers an online char whose guild name is momentarily empty. The 24 h purse memory (funds) is the part that actually keeps wealth steady. Claim "wealth dropping when vendors offline" is only partly addressed; the test (`TestRankStockKeepsOfflineOwners`) feeds `rankStockOf` a pre-merged owner map and so does not exercise the merge at all.
