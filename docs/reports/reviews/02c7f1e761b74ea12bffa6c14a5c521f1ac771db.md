## Findings

### major (improvement, not a regression): lv80+ bots never get an emergency stack, and Red alone is a poor emergency potion (town.go emergencyPot ~207, onDeal ~1408)
Player view (Constitution 2.1): a lv85 bot with 9,700 HP and 3,000z buys 25-60 Red. A Red heals 55 HP, 0.6% of max HP; in a
bad fight 9 turns of Red do not out-heal one boss hit, so for survival the Red stock is almost useless at that HP; it only
tops up between fights. A real player at 3,000z would buy the Red minimum (250z) and 5 Yellow (900z, ~1,000 HP in 5 drinks,
20 potions of Red worth of healing in five actions) and keep the rest. That mix is clearly better for survival and costs
about the same zeny as the Red top-up above the minimum. The branch tries this (emergency stack), but `emergencyPot` always
picks the strongest level-allowed tier, which for lv80+ is White at 1,200z. The budget check
`(potBudget - hpN*price)/emPr` with the default floor 1,000z gives 0 White (the top-up already spent ~450z), and only a bot
earning over about 8,000z/h (15% budget) gets one. So at lv80+ the emergency stack is dead code in practice, and the 9,700 HP
bot has Red only. It is not worse than `main` (the old code also bought Red there), so I do not block on it. Fix: pick the
emergency tier as the strongest one whose potEmergencyN (or at least 3-5) fit the budget (Yellow when White does not), and
test lv85 at 3,000z buys 25 Red plus Yellows. Add that to the backlog as the next supplies batch.

### minor: emergency purchase ignores the planned arrow and SP spend (town.go ~1408)
`n` uses `Zeny - 500 - hpN*price` but not `arrN*ap` and `spN*spPrice` that potBuyPlan already planned; they come out of the
same remainder. With a large arrow and SP order the packet total can exceed zeny, and "one failed total = nothing bought"
(the comment at town.go:1387). Use the `budget` variable (already net of arrows and SP) instead of `b.Zeny-potPickReserve`.

### minor: `fitArrowWant` treats any carried arrow stack as "have" (huntfit.go:165)
A stack of 1 Silver Arrow suppresses buying 50 more. Use a count threshold. Low impact, the want is a market bid only.

### minor: `arrow_gain_per_z` 4 is sane
Meaning: 1% damage per 0.25z extra per arrow, e.g. Silver/Fire vs an Undead map (+25%) pays up to 6z extra over a plain Arrow,
Holy vs Undead (+25%) the same. A lv70+ archer's hit is worth well over 4z of kill time per arrow, so 4 is conservative
(buys too little rather than too much). Acceptable default; tunable via tuning.json.

### minor: carried over, unchanged
Potion budget is per restock, not per hour (potbudget.go); `RC_All` cards now score on every map when the flag is on.
