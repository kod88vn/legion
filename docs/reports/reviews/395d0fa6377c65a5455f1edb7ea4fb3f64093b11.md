## Findings

### Major
- M1. `tools/puppet/briefs/market.md:16-32`, engine `legion/guildduty_api.go:131,223-231` and `guildduty.go:guildDirMax=3`.
  A market direction is stored as `SetBy: "leader"`, is announced by the leader bot in guild chat in the leader's own
  voice, and sits in the same leader-set list as the leader's directions. The list is capped at 3 and a new one
  evicts the OLDEST (`ds[len(ds)-3:]`). So the market can silently push out the leader's own direction (any kind, for
  example a `carry`), and the leader mind cannot tell a market direction from its own except by the "market:" note
  convention. The brief only guards against a leader gear/stock direction and says the "cap of three come first"
  without telling the model to count `leader_set` and skip the guild at 3. This conflicts with the intent of
  Constitution 2.5 (influence, not override the leader). The channel does reach leaders as a priority they may
  follow (autopilot yields to leader-set; the leader can clear; 24 h), but eviction is override. Fix: tell the market
  to skip any guild whose `leader_set` already holds 2 or more, to never use `--exclusive`, and preferably give the
  market its own `set_by` (engine) so leaders and the evict order can tell them apart.
- M2. Process: `docs/` convention in `.claude/agents/reviewer.md` wants behaviour changes behind a `tuning.json` flag
  that defaults off. The market starting to set guild directions is new behaviour that is on whenever the market runs
  (no flag). Its effect is bounded (market agent is not running, G21; directions need `guild_duty_on`, default off,
  to move bots, though the leader's guild-chat announcement happens regardless). Acceptable if the owner is told
  and the market stays off until he starts it; a flag would be cleaner.

### Minor
- m1. `tools/puppet/briefing.py:408-411`: `progress.collect(api)` is not wrapped; an unexpected data shape raises and
  loses the whole market briefing. Wrap it and fall back to a `no_data` block.
- m2. `market.md:29-31`: "Never a goal, map or party command to a bot" restates Constitution 2.5 (PR42); point to it
  instead. `DENIED_CTL` already blocks those verbs.
- m3. Class-line counting by class name is approximate (documented in the brief and module); a market that is told
  to use "only its numbers" may over-read it. Acceptable.

## Verdict reasoning
Code (`progress.py`, briefing block, tests) is sound and merge-ready. The brief change lets the market evict a
leader's own directions through the shared 3-slot list (M1). One brief edit fixes the risk; until then the claim
"market influences without overriding the leader" is not true.

verdict: do not merge
