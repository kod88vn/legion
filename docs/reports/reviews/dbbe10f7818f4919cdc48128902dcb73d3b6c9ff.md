## Findings
- major (follow-up, not a regression), partylead.pl:709 with rally_tick ~line 800 (`if (AI::inQueue('attack'))`): "always fights back" holds only for a melee attacker. On a crossing map, fight_back() starts an attack on the nearest attacker at any distance. When a rally is running, rally_tick keeps an attack only if the target is within 3 cells and has hit or missed her, else it does `AI::clear('attack')`. A ranged attacker (archer, caster) beyond 3 cells is therefore dropped every tick, and fight_back clears route/move and re-issues it the next tick. She can stall and be hit during a rally walk or the 90 s wait. This is the owner's old complaint, now limited to rally plus a ranged attacker. A normal goto crossing and melee attackers are covered. Suggest letting rally_tick keep the attack when the target is a current attacker at any distance, or capping the distance in nearest_attacker.
- minor, partylead.pl nearest_attacker (~2948): it uses `dmgToYou || missedYou` with no distance or recency cap, so a monster that hit her once and is now far away can pull her off the route.
- minor, partylead.pl:791-800 (rally gather wait): bounded at 90 s and then `goneAt` is set for the rest of the rally. `gatherAt` and `goneAt` are never reset, so a second absence later in the same rally is not waited for. Cannot strand her: members whose map is unknown are not counted, the wait is skipped on town maps and in the rally town, and the rally times out at 12 min. During the wait route and move are cleared every tick, so she stands, which relies on attackAuto and stick_tick for defence (attackAuto is off on a trivial crossing, see the major finding).
- minor, partylead.pl `map_dense_enough` / mapdensity.txt: the gate cannot skip a map she needs. Needed = 20 x party size. Her fit maps (lv 84): alde_dun02 448 spawns (22 members), gl_church 708 (35), c_tower2 536 (26), gef_fild06 944. Even the smallest, gl_prison, allows 19 members. With the 400 rate (confirmed in `rathena/conf/import/battle_conf.txt:11`) a party of 7-10 is never blocked, so 20 is effectively off for current parties, not harmful. If every row failed, level_map falls back to the nearest-level map ignoring density (last loop), so no stranding. Counts are whole-map sums, not per reachable area.
- minor, partylead.pl portal_back_guard (~2522): `return if AI::inQueue('sellAuto','buyAuto','storageAuto')` lifts the portal hold for the whole shop task. Acceptable (fixes the 10x/s re-route); a town-run to another map still follows the normal guard afterwards.
- Behaviour defaults: all new behaviour is on by default with no tuning flag (reviewer.md asks for a flag defaulting off), but this is bug-fix behaviour for Ayla (AY21 fixes deploy at once) and DENSITY_PER_MEMBER 0 / `partylead_stick` exist as constants, so not blocking.
- No effect on other parties: all changes are in `openkore-ayla/`; `openkore` and `openkore-qa` configs are untouched.

No blockers.
