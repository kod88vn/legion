## Findings

### Major
1. `legion/ai.go:538` with `ai.go:340` and `bot.go:1592` (behaviour change, harms the guild brewer): `sessionEnd` is set once
   at login and never refreshed. The "calling it a day" trip fires whenever `now > sessionEnd-4min && !isTown && trip == nil
   && !alwaysOnline`. After the new re-pick sends a brewer (ShouldBeOnline true, sessionEnd in the past) out of town to a
   hunting ground, the first step onto a non-town map starts "calling it a day" again, the bot returns home, lands on
   `idle/auto`, and `idleAutoNeedsGoal` re-picks again. Result: an endless town/field round trip that never hunts, which is
   worse than the old idle in town and produces churn on every brewer after its window ends. No test covers the loop (the
   tests call `idleAutoNeedsGoal` only, never think + sessionEnd). Fix: clear or extend `b.sessionEnd` when the re-pick is
   taken (the bot has been judged to stay online), or exclude the trip for held-open bots.

### Minor
2. The pinned and alwaysOnline arms are almost dead code. `bot.go:1594-1595` zeroes `sessionEnd` for pin/leader at login, and
   `ai.go:340` excludes `alwaysOnline`, so these never reach "calling it a day". Only a pin or leader role acquired after
   login can reach the idle. Ayla's party members (pinned) are therefore not affected either way: no day-end wander-off,
   and no change to AY03/AY18 behaviour. If they ever did sit in that idle, re-picking is what AY18 wants (any idle is a bug),
   so the arm is right in intent; it is just not the likely wolfgirl cause. Keep the notes as written.
3. Should a brewer farm? Unclear from `docs/rules/economy.md` and `alchemy.go` (brewing runs in `alchemyThink` and is not a
   goal; `alchShopClose` ignores the goal). Re-picking a hunt goal is plausible (a brewer needs herbs and zeny), but it is a
   new default behaviour the owner did not ask for and there is no tuning flag. Settle after major 1; ideally leave the
   brewer in town (its crafting place) rather than hunt.
4. `b.pin` is read without a lock in `idleAutoNeedsGoal`/`probeIdleAuto`: safe, every caller path is `think()` which holds
   `b.mu` (ai.go:147); `pinned()` would deadlock there. Lock order b.mu then guildLeaders is already used at ai.go:340.
