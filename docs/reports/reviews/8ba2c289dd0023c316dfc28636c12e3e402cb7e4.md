## Blocker

1. Branch is stale and does not merge with main. `git merge-tree HEAD main` conflicts in `legion/lfg.go`. Main's PT35 (dd1ab44, 5290468) replaced `abs(c.lv-P.avg) > lfgLvBand` in lfgFits with `lfgLvFit/lfgLvFits` (partyLvOK + mapExpPoor); the constant `lfgLvBand` is gone. `lfgFitWhy` (lfg.go ~1000-1040 on the branch) rebuilds the old check, so after a naive merge it fails to compile or silently counts the wrong "band". Rebase onto main, split "band" into the two new reasons (spread vs exp-poor) and re-run tests.

## Major

2. The counters miss the filters that most plausibly collapsed party forming, so they will not locate the cause. `lfgGather` (lfg.go ~1150-1180) only tallies `social` and `no_map`. The big `ok` gate (online, fgoal set, Goal.Mode=="hunt", Goal.Set prefix "auto", no pin/vend/trip, not dead, not vendor, lv>=5), the `partyOf(b) != nil` skip, the 3 min `leftAt` cooldown, and every party-side skip (human party, full, town map, isRestoring, bossActive, leader offline, n==0) are not counted. A bot stuck on a non-"auto" goal (the very bug the disband fix addresses) is invisible. Add a per-tick reason tally for these and a gauge of candidates / open parties per gather, so the 49% -> 8-12% share can be read as a breakdown of online bots.
3. The rejection counts are pair counts, not bot counts. In lfgMatch (lfg.go ~1296-1390) one candidate is counted once per open party per tick (and again in the form step), so numbers scale with parties x candidates x ticks and are not comparable to "online bots". Also a "map" rejection is only counted when the candidate would have out-scored the current best, so it is biased low. Say so in /api/lfg or count distinct bots per tick.

## Real collapse cause (not found by the fixer): hypotheses

The branch finds no cause. The window 2026-11-14 20:00 to 2026-11-15 02:00 on main contains these commits touching lfg.go/party.go/partypersist.go/humanparty.go (all marked hypothesis for the collapse):

- hypothesis A, c2b1e68 (22:06, gear-aware map choice, partyFitLv/bestPartySpot use powerLv plus a measured kills/deaths step of -3 / +2-3 levels): parties sit on lower, different maps, so `P.mapOKFor(c)` rejects more joiners and the 8 min step shifts maps often. The offline test (comment 9) did not cover this step. Check `join_rejects "map ..."`.
- hypothesis B, 5a12941 (23:54, PT16 2 h map commitment in leaderGoalSync, lfg.go) with fc914d6/Ayla side: a party commits to a map for 2 h even when it stops fitting joiners; no relocation toward joiners, "map" and "hops" rejections rise. Combined with the party town runs (57b2db0, 7f08ad6): a party in town has `isTown(p.Map)` and is dropped from `ps` in lfgGather, so parties on a town run or Kafra wait take no members; and EC36 errands/trips set `b.trip`/`pin`, excluding bots from `cs`.
- hypothesis C, 7002bd6 (22:15 party-stay 20 cells), 3ad717c (23:03 fight in place), ebed06d, 20a884d/25fe2ea (healer sticks to the leader), 245242c (rally/Kafra park): members stuck waiting or fighting in place get dissolved/left, so the share of bots in a party falls and `members_left` / `parties_dissolved` rise; bots coming out via `leavesParty` (ai.go ~2515) or `disband` with Goal "party-restore"/"help" are locked out by the `Goal.Set` prefix "auto" gate, which is exactly what ce3d08d fixes for two of the sets. This cause is old (initial commit) but restarts during the night (restoring parties, "party-restore") make it bite more; correlate with engine restart times.
- hypothesis D, 4a03b76 (23:56, warp graph from server npc files, +537 warps, ferry edges): changes `mapHops`/route plans; more edges should help `hops` but also change routes and `townHops`, so cheap to check by comparing `hops`/`beginner` rejections before and after. Unlikely cause.
- hypothesis E, 20bcbdf / c2ea3ae (guild duty goal Kind "guild", Starveil member parties): `goalStatus` and `c.g.serves(P.m)` treat a guild-duty candidate as "serves" only on its block's map; guild_duty_on defaults off, so only relevant if it was switched on.
- not in the window but on main since: PT35 (dd1ab44, 2026-11-15 15:02) widened the +-10 level band after bug b1794772058nfq (lv83-90 bots without partners). That is a likely contributor to the high-level share and is not in this branch's base, see blocker 1.

Neither of the changed lfg.go hunks in the window (20bcbdf goalStatus, 5a12941 leaderGoalSync) changes candidate gathering directly; lfgGather and lfgFits are unchanged between the initial commit and PT35. So the cause is more likely in what bots do before gathering (goal, trip, pin, party state) than in the matching rules. Best next step after a rebase: count the `ok`-gate reasons (finding 2) over one engine run and read `join_rejects` split by band.

verdict: do not merge
