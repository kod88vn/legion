## Findings

### major
1. `legion/buildmilestones.go:171-188` (gearGateOf), EC39, PR03. The cap is binary and very large: any unmet piece in band N drops the ceiling to the end of band N-1, and band 21-40 unmet means 20, even for a lv59 bot. On the snapshot roster (lv21-60, n=2948) 2728 fail the weapon and 220 the armor milestone; 1842 of 1844 bots at lv40-59 and all 94 at lv60 are gated, 1756 of them 20 or more levels under their base level (average effective level 20 against 39 without the gate, for lv40+). Deployed with `gear_gate_on` true this sends nearly the whole bot world to beginner maps in one tick: lv40-60 players on lv20 fields give no exp and a normal society does not behave that way (PR03). Needs a ramp (cap only a few levels under base per missing band, or a proportional rating), or the milestones need to be reachable by the roster first.
2. `legion/fairgate.go:60-80` against `gearscore.go:151` (cap) - fair gate uses base level, the gate caps the effective level. For a lv50 bot capped at 20 every spot of `SpotsFor(20)` has monsters under base-15=35, so `spotsFor` empties and takes the single best-fair-share spot (deterministic: same spot for every bot of that band), ignoring crowd and room (`mapHasRoom`/`mapLoad` are not consulted in the fallback), then `logFairFallback` fires once per bot per hour. Result: hundreds of gated bots funnel to one map, and the guide-spot path (`strategy.go:183`) drops all its spots for the same reason. `TestGearGatedBotNeverIdles` only asserts `len(spots) > 0`, which passes through exactly this fallback, so it hides the problem. The cap and the fair gate are structurally in conflict for any deep gate (gate more than ~15 under base); the fair gate needs to be judged at the gated level for a gated bot, or the fallback must spread by crowd.
3. `legion/ai.go:852-855`, `buildmilestones.go` - "can earn its gear" is not delivered. All 28 distinct milestone items across the builds (checked with the real data) are the NPC route items of the build (`inRoute`), 7,000-65,000z each, with no drop route (`pieceFarms` empty for all). So the forced drop-map goal can never fire for a milestone piece. The forced goal also does not target the unmet milestone: `nextBuildPiece` (buildgear.go:685) picks by weighted gain over every slot (heads, garments, shoes, cards) and the gate only forces the drop map of whatever piece it returned. A capped bot has to earn 22,500-65,000z (e.g. Bastard Sword 22.5k, Two-Handed Sword 60k, Hunter Bow 64k, Arc Wand 45k, Full Plate 80k) on maps 20+ levels below its base; with finding 2's single spot this can take many hours and nothing guarantees the zeny goes to the milestone piece first. The cards milestone (41-60: 1 card, 61+: 2) has no goal at all in `nextBuildPiece`, so a bot with the 41-60 weapon and armor but no card sits at 40 until loot happens to give one.
4. `legion/ai.go`, `legion/party.go` (whole files) and `legion/gearscore.go`: the commit rewrote line endings. `ai.go` and `party.go` went CRLF to LF (5,000+ changed lines; the real change is 8 and 4 lines) and `gearscore.go` went LF to CRLF (270 lines of the base are LF). Hides the diff from review (`git diff -w --ignore-cr-at-eol` is needed) and will conflict with every other open branch touching those files (for example party-collapse). Restore the original endings.
