# Guilds

One line per rule: ID, statement, date. Owner words in `archive.md`. Design details: `docs/design/REDESIGN.md` section 3G.

- GD01. Guilds compete on rank and wealth; their leaders are frenemies who deal and cooperate when it suits and compete the rest
  of the time. Guild wars come later. (2026-11-14)
- GD02. A member generally follows its guild's direction as long as the task is safe for it and it can do it, for about 2 hours a
  day, not all its time. A member who does not contribute risks being kicked. No bounty mechanism. (2026-11-14)
- GD03. Each guild leader's account is always online. The leader looks after the whole guild: member gear, guild resources
  (zeny, items, storage), parties. Driven by its puppet-master agent (Haiku) when running. (2026-11-13)
- GD04. Guild leaders are the server's best players: they form the right party and level fast, and sit on the leaderboard. (2026-11-13)
- GD05. Each guild leader (Starveil included) tracks guild stock and every member's development and keeps supplies ready before
  members need them (potions, common and build-specific gear). It assigns dedicated farmers with high solo survivability, DS
  Hunters first, to hunt what the guild needs. Stock is kept in guild storage, or in the leader's or treasurer's storage while guild storage is not unlocked. (2026-11-14)
- GD06. Every guild sets a small EXP tax on member positions to level up; the tax applies to every member, no human
  exception (G25, 2026-11-14); guild skill points go to Guild Extension first, then
  the useful skills. (2026-11-14)
- GD07. Every guild has a Merchant-line member (Vending, Discount, Overcharge) as its trader: vends surplus at the Prontera
  market, sells to NPCs with Overcharge, buys what the guild needs (from player shops, #trade, and NPCs with Discount) for the guild stock. (2026-11-14)
- GD08. Every bot aims for the Creamy Card (Teleport): an early step in every build's gear path, high priority and high demand in
  the market; bots use Teleport to travel and escape. (2026-11-14)
- GD09. Every guild member (Starveil included; only Ayla's party is fixed) has a loyalty that grows when the guild takes care of
  it and falls when ignored. Leaders send gear or money to members at risk of leaving. Members ask for help in guild chat and
  guild mates who can, help. Loyalty and friendship decide whether a member stays. (2026-11-14)
- GD10. A few lv40+ bots now and then reset into another build variant, rare and spread over time, never Ayla's party, guild
  leaders, or guild alchemists and traders. (2026-11-14)
- CT01. Items go free to party members and to guild mates (spares), or at a heavy discount; see `chat.md`.
